title: Feeding a starter
minutes: 3
---

Weigh equal parts starter, water, and flour. Stir until no dry flour is left, cover the jar loosely, and leave it somewhere warm.

A healthy starter doubles in four to six hours and smells sweet and a little sour. If yours is slow, feed it twice a day for a few days and keep it out of drafts. When you bake less often, store the jar in the fridge and feed it once a week.
